# ASL numbers 1-10

Practice the ASL numbers 1 to 10 with your phone's (or laptop's) camera. Each number is checked live in the browser:
hold it until the ring fills; if it looks like another number, the page says which, and what to change. 10 is a
thumbs-up with a small twist. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

The numbers grader was fitted on FSboard (Google; Georg et al., 2024; CC BY 4.0): 92 Deaf signers fingerspelling
phone numbers, the digits cut out by forced alignment. Hands are read with MediaPipe (Google). The glove pictures are
renders of the signers' average hands. Research prototype; grading is approximate and can be wrong.
Source: the `numbers` branch of physical-tutor.
